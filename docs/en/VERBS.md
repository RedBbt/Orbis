# ORBIS — The Verb

*Derived from the German documentation, which is authoritative (see TRANSLATION_POLICY.md). Describes Orbis Grammar 0.9.3.*

---

## 1. Basic principle (§14)

> **Root + tense vowel + person ending**

The verb has neither a class consonant nor a theme vowel; the nominal formula from §8 does
not apply to it. It agrees with the subject in person and number alone.

### 1.1 Person endings (§14)

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **-m** | **-men** |
| 2nd | **-ş** | **-şen** |
| 3rd | **-t** | **-ten** |

The plural is throughout the singular + **-en** (§28).

### 1.2 Tense vowels (§15)

| Tense | Vowel |
|---|---|
| Present | **-a-** |
| Past | **-o-** |
| Future | **-ai-** |

### 1.3 The non-finite forms and the imperative (§14)

| Form | Formation | Example (*mel-*) |
|---|---|---|
| Infinitive | root + **-ex** | melex |
| Participle | root + **-ut** | melut |
| Imperative, familiar sg. | bare root | **Mel!** |
| Imperative, pl. | root + **-eñ** | **Meleñ!** |
| Imperative, polite | 3rd pl. present | **Melaten!** |

## 3. Infinitive, participle and imperative in detail

### 3.1 Infinitive **-ex** (§14)

The infinitive stands in the verbal bracket at the end of the sentence (§16.1, §17.3).
Attestations: *melex* (§14), *soñex* (§25.1: *Vim xa valnam soñex, grali xla kirva vran
luid est.*), *milkex* (§16.1: *Vim valnam xnan melnan milkex.*), *vandex* (§16.1: *Şet
dolmaş nunda vandex.*), *zaubex* (§25.1: *Valnaten şevar vin zaubex?*).

### 3.2 Participle **-ut** (§14)

| Form | Use | Attestation |
|---|---|---|
| *melut* | basic attestation of the formation | §14 |
| *şunargut* | predicative in the statal passive: *Xna breun şunargut est.* | §16.3 |
| *traivut* → *traivute* | nominalized (N-C, §12.5): *Kilna est xna traivute.* | §12.5, §25.2 |

**[REGELUNKLARHEIT]** (rule ambiguity) **U-02:** Whether the participle may be declined
**attributively** ("the found book" → *xna traivutna vresto*?) is stated by no rule. §14
defines the formation, §16.3 uses it predicatively, §12.5 nominalizes it — the attributive
use is unregulated (test corpus 0.1, test 051).

### 3.3 The three imperatives (§14)

| Addressee | Formation | Attestation |
|---|---|---|
| familiar sg. | bare root | *Mel!* |
| pl. | root + **-eñ** | *Meleñ!* |
| polite (*şevar*) | 3rd pl. present | *Melaten!* |

The polite form is consistent: *şevar* generally requires the verb in the 3rd person
plural (§13.2: *Şevar melaten.*).

**Supporting -e:** If the root ends in a group inadmissible under §5.3, an **-e** is added
in the imperative. The grammar's attestation is *Suvre!* (§14). In the same paragraph §14
states that modal verbs — and *suvr-* "to like" is one (§16.1) — do not normally form an
imperative.

**[REGELUNKLARHEIT] U-12:** The supporting-e rule checks §5.3, not §5.1. The roots
*dremn-, prens-, vlent-* have codas conforming to §5.3 (*mn, ns, nt*); their imperatives
*Dremn!, Prens!, Vlent!* would however have the syllable shape KKVKK, which §5.1 does not
list (→ K-01). Whether the supporting -e is to apply here cannot be decided.

## 4. The eight irregular verbs (§15.2)

> Eight frequent verbs preserve an older vowel grade in the past:
> **ablaut stem + linking vowel -e- + person ending** (§15.2)

### 4.1 Overview (§15.2)

| Meaning | Infinitive | Present 3 sg. | Past 3 sg. | Future 3 sg. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| to be | esex | **est** | **vot** | vait |
| to have | nuvex | **nut** | **novet** | nuvait |
| to become | vurnex | **vurt** | **voret** | vurnait |
| to go | melex | melat | **molet** | melait |
| to come | vandex | vandat | **vendet** | vandait |
| to make | nargex | nargat | **norget** | nargait |
| to know | zavex | zavat | **zovet** | zavait |
| to give | dalvex | dalvat | **dolvet** | dalvait |

### 4.2 [REGELUNKLARHEIT] U-01 — the formula does not cover three verbs

The description in §15.2 ("preserve an older vowel grade **in the past**", formula "ablaut
stem + linking vowel -e- + person ending") agrees only partly with the grammar's own
tables:

1. **esex** is suppletive throughout. The present *em/eş/est/emen/eşen/esten* fits no
   formula (3 sg. *est*), the past is *vo-* + ending **without** linking vowel *-e-*
   (*vot*, not \**vovet*), the future has its own stem *vai-*.
2. **nuvex** and **vurnex** additionally have contracted **present** stems *nu-* and
   *vur-* (*num, nut, vurt* instead of \**nuvam, nuvat, vurnat*) — a deviation in the
   present, of which the formula does not speak at all.
3. Only *mel-, vand-, narg-, zav-, dalv-* correspond exactly to the description in the
   text.

The **forms** are fully attested and therefore checkable; only the description of the rule
is unclear. Nothing is corrected and nothing is decided here.

## 5. Modal verbs (§16.1)

| Root | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **valn-** | can, to be able to |
| **dolm-** | must, to have to |
| **vlek-** | may, to be allowed to |
| **tirn-** | shall, ought to |
| **nest-** | to want |
| **suvr-** | to like |

They are conjugated regularly (§14/§15). The construction is a verbal bracket:

> Conjugated modal verb in position 2, main verb in the **infinitive at the end of the
> sentence**.

Attestations: *Vim valnam xnan melnan milkex.* — I can see the way. (§16.1) ·
*Şet dolmaş nunda vandex.* — You must come today. (§16.1) ·
*Vim xa valnam soñex, grali xla kirva vran luid est.* (§25.1) ·
*Valnaten şevar vin zaubex?* (§25.1) ·
*Vim valnam nunda zva xraş velkraş dun xlaş kavlaş melex.* (§17.3).

The negation *xa* is placed before the finite modal verb, not before the infinitive
(§18.3, attestation §25.1 above).

**[REGELUNKLARHEIT] U-03 — modal verb without infinitive.** Whether a modal verb may
stand with a direct object and without an infinitive ("I like the word" → *Vim suvram
xnan taisan*) is not regulated. §16.1 describes only the bracket construction. No
attestation in 0.9.3 shows such a use (test corpus 0.1, test 111).

**[REGELKONFLIKT]** (rule conflict) **K-05 — modal verb in the subordinate clause.** §16.1
requires the main-verb infinitive "at the end of the sentence", §17.2 requires the
**finite** verb at the end in the subordinate clause. In "…, that the man must go to the
city tomorrow" both compete for the same position; whether *… melex dolmat* or *… dolmat
melex* is correct is decided by no rule. **Not a single example in 0.9.3 contains a modal
verb in a subordinate clause** (test corpus 0.1, tests 103 and 105). Nothing is guessed
here.

On the imperative: §14 states that modal verbs do not normally form an imperative.

## 6. Possibility form *mai* (§16.2)

The particle **mai** stands immediately before the finite verb; *mai* and the finite verb
form **one** sentence position.

Attestations: *Vim mai melam.* — I would go. (§16.2) ·
*Tund vim vra vlaidra valru mai em, mai traivam vim xlan eirden.* — If I were a big man, I
would find the world. (§16.2, §25.1).

The second attestation shows *mai* both in the subordinate clause (*mai em*) and in the
main clause (*mai traivam*). Because *mai* + verb counts as one position, the V2 rule
remains intact. In Orbis Manus this modality corresponds to the closing sign "verwehend"
(drifting away) (§26.7) — that is a script rule and not a grammar rule.

## 7. Passive (§16.3)

Formed with the prefix **şu-**:

| Form | Formation | Attestation |
|---|---|---|
| Dynamic passive | *şu-* + finite verb | *Xna breun şunargat.* — The house is being built. |
| Statal passive | *şu-* + participle *-ut* + *esex* | *Xna breun şunargut est.* — The house is built. |

The prefix remains unstressed (§23: *şu-NAR-gat*) and does not count as part of the
lexical stem (§3.5).

**[REGELLÜCKE]** (rule gap) **L-05 — agent in the passive.** "The house is built **by the
man**" **cannot be formed** in 0.9.3. §16.3 regulates the prefix alone; no preposition is
designated for the agent. *ven* + dative (§19) would suggest itself but is nowhere fixed —
and is not fixed here (test corpus 0.1, tests 137 and 138).

## 8. Further prefixes on the verb (§21.2)

| Prefix | Meaning | Attestation |
|---|---|---|
| **şu-** | passive | *şunargat* |
| **re-** | again | *revandat* |
| **dra-** | completely, through | *dramilkat* |
| **su-** | half | *suluidra* (on the adjective) |

*xa-* "opposite, absence" (§21.2, *xaselvra*) is to be distinguished from the negation
particle *xa* (§18.3); likewise *dral* "through" is exclusively a preposition and *dra-*
exclusively a prefix (§19).
