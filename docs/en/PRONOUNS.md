# ORBIS — The Pronouns

*Derived from the German documentation, which is authoritative (see TRANSLATION_POLICY.md). Describes Orbis Grammar 0.9.3.*

---

## 1. Two bodies of material with very different degrees of maturity

§13 of the grammar falls into two unequal halves:

| Area | State of regulation | Findings |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal pronouns** (§13.1–§13.3) | fully declined, polite form and possessive regulated | none (audit §9.1) |
| **Remaining pronouns** (§13.4) | only a list of forms, without declension and without syntax | L-02, L-04, L-06, plus K-02 |

This chapter treats both halves separately. Sections 2–4 describe regulated material,
section 5 the open areas. For the open areas the following applies throughout: the missing
forms are **not** supplied here and **not** inferred by analogy.

---

## 2. Personal pronouns (§13.1)

Nine series of forms, each in four cases. The table stands complete in §13.1; it is
reproduced here unchanged.

| | Nom | Acc | Dat | Gen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | **vim** | vin | viş | vis |
| you (familiar sg.) | **şet** | şen | şeş | şes |
| you (polite) | **şevar** | şevan | şevaş | şevas |
| he | **ro** | ron | roş | ros |
| she | **lo** | lon | loş | los |
| it | **no** | non | noş | nos |
| we | **viñ** | viñan | viñaş | viñas |
| you (pl.) | **şeñ** | şeñan | şeñaş | şeñas |
| they | **oñ** | oñan | oñaş | oñas |

### 2.1 Observations on the table

The case markers are the same as with the noun (§8): **nom —, acc -n, dat -ş, gen -s**.
In the three plural series (*viñ, şeñ, oñ*) and in the polite form an **-a-** appears
before the marker (*viñan, şeñaş, oñas, şevan*) — the same place at which §9 puts the echo
vowel with the noun. The nominative **şevar** ends in *-r*, the oblique forms build on
*şeva-*.

This is a description of the attested table, **not a rule of derivation**: §13.1 gives the
36 forms as a list and formulates no pattern of formation. Anyone wishing to form new
pronouns would have no rule — the class is closed, the question does not arise in 0.9.3.

The form **oñ** (they) has the syllable shape VK, which §5.1 does not list — a partial
case of the syllable-shape conflict **K-01**. This concerns phonotactics, not declension.

| Form | Attestation | Translation |
|---|---|---|

| vin (acc sg 1) | *Vim milkam vin.* (test 032) | I see myself. |
| viş (dat sg 1) | *Vim dalvam viş xlan nauşen.* (test 034) | I give myself time. |
| vis (gen sg 1) | *xna breun vis* (§13.3) | my house |
| şet (nom sg 2) | *Kelnan vreston leşnaş şet?* (test 079) | Which book do you read? |
| lo (nom sg 3 f) | *Lo saivat xlan taiven.* (test 029) | She loves the language. |

| oñas (gen pl 3) | *xrañ valruñ oñas* (§13.3) | their men |

## 3. Polite form (§13.2)

> **şevar requires the verb in the 3rd person plural.**

The polite form is thus not merely one more pronoun but entails a shift of agreement: the
pronoun stands in the singular of address, the verb in the plural ending *-ten / -men /
-şen* (§14).

| Address | Attestation | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| familiar | *Şet melaş.* (§13.2) | You go. |
| polite | *Şevar melaten.* (§13.2) | You go. (polite) |
| polite, question with modal verb | *Valnaten şevar vin zaubex?* (§25.1; test 114) | Can you hear me? (polite) |

The grammar gives the polite form without a plural counterpart: §13.1 names no separate
form for the polite address of several persons; *şevar* covers both, because the verb
stands in the plural anyway.

**Forms of greeting** stand in §13.2 immediately beside the polite form:
**selvai** (hello) · **melai** (goodbye) · **selves** (thank you) · **praum** (please).

## 4. Possessive (§13.3)

Orbis has **no possessive pronouns of its own**. The role is taken over by the genitive of
the personal pronoun, and this is **placed after** the noun:

| Orbis | English | Source |
|---|---|---|
| *xna breun vis* | my house | §13.3 |
| *xla sarla şes* | your woman | §13.3 |
| *xrañ valruñ oñas* | their men | §13.3 |
| *Nunda melam vim dun xnaş breuneş vis.* | Today I go to my house. | §25.1 |

The possessive is **invariable**: *vis* remains *vis*, whatever case the noun it refers to
stands in (*dun xnaş breuneş vis* — head noun in the dative, possessive unchanged). The
case is marked on the article and on the noun, not on the postposed genitive.

> **[REGELLÜCKE L-01]** (rule gap) **— touched.** For the **simple** possessive case the
> position is regulated ("placed after", §13.3). Not regulated is the **stacking** of
> genitive attribute and possessive: "the book of my friend" could read *xna vresto xras
> velkras vis* or be ordered differently; whether *vis* refers to *vresto* or to *velkra*
> cannot be seen from the form. Test corpus 039 is therefore rated as [REGELLÜCKE].
> Equally open: the position of the genitive attribute with a pure noun genitive
> (test 036, *xna breun xras valrus* or *xras valrus xna breun*).

### 4.1 Reflexive use of the 1st and 2nd person

The test corpus forms reflexive sentences of the 1st and 2nd person by means of the
ordinary personal pronoun: *Vim milkam vin* ("I see myself", test 032), *Vim dalvam viş
xlan nauşen* ("I give myself time", test 034). Both sentences are formally rule-conforming
— they use only §13.1. Whether the grammar **provides for** this, or whether *se* (§13.4)
should stand instead, it does not say; see L-04 in section 5.2.

## 5. The remaining pronouns (§13.4) — open material

§13.4 consists of a single table. It lists base forms and nothing else: no declension, no
syntax, no rule of agreement.

| Kind | Forms according to §13.4 |
|---|---|
| Demonstrative (this) | **kilra / killa / kilna** |
| Demonstrative (that) | **dolra / dolla / dolna** |
| Reflexive | **se** |
| Relative | **fai** |
| Indefinite | **kaun** (one) · **kelsu** (someone) · **xakaun** (nobody) · **kelte** (something) · **xakelte** (nothing) |

### 5.1 Demonstratives — [REGELLÜCKE L-06]

The threefold series *-ra / -la / -na* is the A series of the class consonants (§7) and
thus formally identical with article (§11) and attributive adjective (§12.1). A pattern of
declension is however **not** derived from this in §13.4.

> **[REGELLÜCKE L-06] Declension of the demonstratives not defined.**
> §13.4 names only the base forms. Neither case nor plural forms are given, and whether
> the words serve as an attribute (*killa sarla*) or only as an independent pronoun is not
> stated. "I see this man" cannot be formed with the support of a rule.

**The only attestation in the entire material:** *Kilna est xna traivute.* — "This is that
which is found." (§25.2, short text). What is attested is thus exclusively the
**nominative neuter as an independent pronoun**. Any further form would be speculation.

### 5.2 Reflexive pronoun se — [REGELLÜCKE L-04]

> **[REGELLÜCKE L-04] The case forms and the range of persons of se are undefined.**
> §13.4 lists *se* without any form. It is not stated whether *se* is indeclinable or
> whether it would have case forms (\**sen*, \**seş*), whether it may stand after
> prepositions, and for which persons it holds.

Two test sentences fail on this and are rated as [REGELLÜCKE]:

| Test | English | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| 033 | He sees himself. | *Ro milkat se*(?) — the accusative of *se* is not defined; *Ro milkat ron* would mean "he sees him (someone else)". |
| 035 | She speaks about herself. | *Lo talat span se*(?) — *span* requires dative or accusative (§19); *se* has neither the one form nor the other. |

The gap has two sides: the missing forms **and** the missing range of persons. As long as
it is not decided whether the 1st and 2nd person use *se* at all, the practice described
in section 4.1 (*Vim milkam vin*) also remains without express cover.

### 5.3 Relative pronoun fai — [REGELLÜCKE L-02]

Of all the pronoun findings this is the most consequential: it blocks not one form but a
**whole sentence type**.

> **[REGELLÜCKE L-02] The relative clause is completely unregulated.**
> §13.4 names *fai* as a relative pronoun. Missing are: case forms of *fai*, agreement
> with the antecedent (gender, number), verb position in the relative clause, and the
> position of the relative clause within the whole sentence. §17 does not mention relative
> clauses.

Four test sentences therefore cannot be formed: 100 ("The man who comes is my friend"),
101 ("The woman whom I see speaks" — would require an accusative \**fain*), 102 ("The man
to whom I give the book waits" — would require a dative \**faiş*) and 149 (complex
sentence with an embedded relative clause).

**Homonymy fai / fai.** The same form *fai* is in §20 the subordinating conjunction
"that" — attested in *Vim zavam, fai şet zirna vandaiş* ("I know that you will come
tomorrow", §25.1). Both words stand in the lexicon as separate entries (`fai`
conjunction, `fai` pronoun). The collision is registered as **W-03** and remains open; it
is expressly not resolved by an incidental reformulation (ORBIS_CONSTITUTION Art. 6 names
exactly this case as an example).

### 5.4 Indefinite pronouns — [REGELLÜCKE L-06]

> **[REGELLÜCKE L-06] Declension of the indefinites not defined.**
> As with the demonstratives, only the base forms are listed. The oblique cases ("to see
> someone", "to speak with nobody") lack the form.

**Attestation:** *Xakaun vandat.* — "Nobody comes." (test 088). The sentence is [OK]
because it needs only the nominative; the analysis expressly notes "oblique forms →
L-06". *xakaun* is at the same time an example of the prefix **xa-** (§21.2,
opposite/absence) on the base *kaun*.

**Homonymy kaun / kaun.** The indefinite *kaun* "one" is formally identical with the core
word *kaun* "human being" (§24.1). This too is registered under **W-03**, as are *vran*
(indefinite article M acc / particle "very") and *xa* (negation particle / prefix).

### 5.5 Interrogative pronouns (§18.2) — [REGELLÜCKE L-03]

The interrogative words stand in §18.2, not in §13, but share the problem situation of
§13.4.

| Interrogative | Status |
|---|---|
| **kem** (who), **kelt** (what) | base form only; case forms **not defined** → L-03 |
| **kur, kan, grais, kolm** | used invariably, no question of case |
| **kelra / kella / kelna** (which, m./f./n.) | agree like adjectives — attested: *Kellan sarlan milkoş?* (§18.2) |

> **[REGELLÜCKE L-03] "Whom?" (acc.), "To whom?" (dat.), "Whose?" cannot be formed.**
> §18.2 lists *kem* and *kelt* only in the base form. Neither case forms (\**kemen*,
> \**kemeş*, \**kemes*) nor indeclinability are laid down. Tests 074, 075 and 076 are
> therefore [REGELLÜCKE].

With *kelra/kella/kelna* the situation is better but not complete: only the **accusative**
is attested (*Kellan sarlan*). Test corpus 079 (*Kelnan vreston leşnaş şet?*) and 080
(*Kelnaş şirneş dalvaş şet xnan brasin?*) follow the adjective paradigm §12.1 by analogy;
080 is expressly noted as a borderline case relating to L-03.

### 5.6 [REGELKONFLIKT K-02] — killa, dolla, kella against the junction rule

> **[REGELKONFLIKT K-02]** (rule conflict) **The pronoun forms with l+l contradict §21.4.**
> §21.4 lets two identical consonants merge at a morpheme junction and expressly declares
> this to hold for "compounds, derivations and **endings** alike" — attested in
> *mel + la → mela*, *tal + la → tala*, *şaln + na → şalna*. The feminine series of the
> pronouns, by contrast, keeps the junction unmerged:

| Form | Junction | Expected under §21.4 |
|---|---|---|
| **killa** (§13.4) | kil + la | \*kila |
| **dolla** (§13.4) | dol + la | \*dola |
| **kella / kellan** (§18.2) | kel + la | \*kela / \*kelan |

Either these forms are exceptions to §21.4 — in which case no rule says so — or they would
have to read differently. Neither can be decided in 0.9.3; the grammar is frozen, the
conflict stands. On the counterpart among the numerals (*telnxelmmern*, K-03) see
`docs/de/WORTBILDUNG.md`.

The masculine and the neuter series are not affected: *kilra, kilna, dolra, dolna, kelra,
kelna* have two different consonants at the junction.
