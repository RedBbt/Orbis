# ORBIS — The Noun

*Derived from the German documentation, which is authoritative (see TRANSLATION_POLICY.md). Describes Orbis Grammar 0.9.3.*

---

## 1. Basic principle (§6)

Orbis has three genders: **masculine, feminine, neuter**. The gender is visible on the
word itself; in about 90 % of all nouns it is immediately recognizable from the ending
(§6).

> **Class consonant + theme vowel**

The **class consonant** carries the gender, the **theme vowel** belongs permanently to the
word and provides variety of sound (§6). *valru* is masculine because *-r-* is masculine;
the *-u-* is pure word identity and has no grammatical function.

Mnemonic of the grammar (§7.3): **r-k-d masculine · l-v-m feminine · n-s-t neuter.**

---

## 2. The 45 regular gender endings (§7)

Nine class consonants × five theme vowels give 45 endings.

| Gender | Subclass A | Subclass B | Subclass C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masculine | **r** | **k** | **d** |
| Feminine | **l** | **v** | **m** |
| Neuter | **n** | **s** | **t** |

### 2.1 The complete table of endings

The columns are the five theme vowels (§7.1–§7.3). In brackets stands a word attested in
the vocabulary of 0.9.3; "—" means: the ending is regular, but attested by no word in the
frozen vocabulary (§24.2–§24.4).

| Class | -a | -e | -i | -o | -u |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M-A** (r) | -ra (*velkra* friend) | -re (*zaldre* day) | -ri — | -ro — | -ru (*valru* man) |
| **M-B** (k) | -ka — | -ke — | -ki (*larki* stone) | -ko (*vlaiko* wind) | -ku (*narku* dog) |
| **M-C** (d) | -da (*morda* storm) | -de — | -di (*luvandi* river) | -do (*vrondo* mountain) | -du — |
| **F-A** (l) | -la (*sarla* woman) | -le — | -li — | -lo (*zenlo* flower) | -lu — |
| **F-B** (v) | -va (*kirva* night) | -ve — | -vi (*gluvi* flame) | -vo — | -vu — |
| **F-C** (m) | -ma (*soruma* memory) | -me — | -mi — | -mo — | -mu — |
| **N-A** (n) | -na (*melna* way) | -ne (*milne* eye) | -ni — | -no (*verno* heart) | -nu (*skelnu* sky) |
| **N-B** (s) | -sa (*taisa* word) | -se — | -si (*brasi* bread) | -so (*pliso* feather) | -su — |
| **N-C** (t) | -ta — | -te (*vlaidte* the big thing, §12.5) | -ti — | -to (*vresto* book) | -tu (*veltu* year) |

A count against §24.2–§24.4 and §12.5: 24 of the 45 endings are attested by at least one
word, 21 are regular but unattested. That is an observation on the frozen vocabulary, not
a rule — all 45 endings are equally valid under §7. Test corpus 0.1 checks them completely
on the explicitly marked test stem *pren-* (Appendix 1, 45 form series; these forms are not
words of the vocabulary).

### 2.2 Meaning of the subclasses (§7.4)

| Subclass | Tendency according to §7.4 |
|---|---|
| **A** (r / l / n) | basic, frequent, old everyday words |
| **B** (k / v / s) | concrete, derived, more recent words |
| **C** (d / m / t) | abstract, traditional, elevated words |

§7.4 expressly calls this a tendency, not a fixed rule; the definitive function of the
subclasses is marked in §7.4 and §30 as **[NOCH ZU ENTSCHEIDEN]** (still to be decided).

## 3. The four cases (§8)

> **Stem + class consonant + theme vowel + case marker**

| Case | Marker |
|---|---|
| Nominative | — |
| Accusative | **-n** |
| Dative | **-ş** |
| Genitive | **-s** |

### 3.1 Full table *valru* "man" (M-A)

Singular according to §8, plural according to §9:

| Case | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | **valru** | **valruñ** |
| Accusative | **valrun** | **valruñun** |
| Dative | **valruş** | **valruñuş** |
| Genitive | **valrus** | **valruñus** |

All eight forms stand literally in §8 and §9.

### 3.2 The same series feminine and neuter (§8, §9)

| Case | *sarla* (F-A) sg | *sarla* pl | *milne* (N-A) sg | *milne* pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | sarla | sarlañ | milne | milneñ |
| Accusative | sarlan | sarlañan | milnen | milneñen |
| Dative | sarlaş | sarlañaş | milneş | milneñeş |
| Genitive | sarlas | sarlañas | milnes | milneñes |

The case marker is the same in all three genders and all nine subclasses; the gender
resides in the class consonant alone.

Attested sentences: *Xra valru milkat xran narkun.* (nom + acc, §25.1) ·
*Xla veiş dolvet xnaş şirneş xnan brasin.* (dat + acc, §25.1) ·
*Xla soruma xlas nauşes vran tolm vaşnat.* (genitive, §25.1).

**[REGELLÜCKE]** (rule gap) **L-01:** The **position** of the genitive attribute is not
fixed. §8 defines the genitive only morphologically, §17 is silent on the position of
attributes. All examples place the genitive attribute after the head (*xla soruma xlas
nauşes*, *xlan luiven xlas eirdes*, §25.1/§25.2); that is not a rule. Equally open is the
order when genitive and possessive are stacked.

## 4. Plural (§9)

> Marker **-ñ-** between theme vowel and case marker, in the oblique cases with an
> **echo vowel**.

The nominative plural has no echo vowel (*valruñ*), the three oblique cases have one
(*valruñun, valruñuş, valruñus*).

**[REGELUNKLARHEIT]** (rule ambiguity) **U-09:** The term "echo vowel" is nowhere defined
in 0.9.3. From the tables it follows that it is the theme vowel of the noun (u→u, a→a,
e→e) and that with article and adjective it is always *a* — but this is not formulated as
a rule; the tables alone carry the definition.

Attested echo vowels:

| Theme vowel | attested plural form | Attestation |
|---|---|---|
| u | *valruñun, valruñuş, valruñus* | §9 |
| a | *sarlañan, sarlañaş, sarlañas* | §9 |
| e | *milneñen, milneñeş, milneñes*; *zaldreñen* | §9; §25.2 |
| i | *larkiñiş* | §25.2 ("şlim xrañaş larkiñiş") |
| o | *vrestoñon* | test corpus 0.1, test 067 |

The forms with *i* and *o* are attested but explicable only by analogy with the tables
(U-09).

**Historical secondary form (§9):** The 15 old core words preserve the plural in **-ei**
(see section 5).

## 5. The 15 core words (§10.2, §10.3, §24.1)

Fifteen true exceptions, exhaustively enumerated. Their gender cannot be read off the
form; it is dictionary knowledge.

| Word | Meaning | Gender | Proto-Orbis | Plural (§24.1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **kaun** | human being | m | \*kau-nu | kaunei |
| **veiş** | mother | f | \*veş-iv | veişei |
| **draun** | father | m | \*drau-nu | draunei |
| **şirn** | child | n | \*şir-ni | şirnei |
| **aul** | water | n | \*au-lu | aulei |
| **xerp** | fire | m | \*xer-pa | xerpei |
| **luiv** | sun | f | \*luv-iv | luivei |
| **grein** | earth, ground | f | \*gre-ina | greinei |
| **nauş** | time | f | \*nau-şa | nauşei |
| **virn** | life | n | \*vir-na | virnei |
| **moks** | death | m | \*mok-sa | moksei |
| **eird** | world | f | \*e-irda | eirdei |
| **şaul** | name | m | \*şa-ulo | şaulei |
| **taiv** | language | f | \*tal-iv | taivei |
| **breun** | house | n | \*breu-na | breunei |

### 5.1 Declension (§10.3)

> **Singular: word + linking vowel -e- + case marker. Plural: word + -ei + case marker.**

| Case | *kaun* (m) | *aul* (n) | *eird* (f) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nom sg | kaun | aul | eird |
| Acc sg | kaunen | aulen | eirden |
| Dat sg | kauneş | auleş | eirdeş |
| Gen sg | kaunes | aules | eirdes |
| Nom pl | kaunei | aulei | eirdei |
| Acc pl | kaunein | aulein | eirdein |
| Dat pl | kauneiş | auleiş | eirdeiş |
| Gen pl | kauneis | auleis | eirdeis |

The nominative singular has no ending. The linking vowel is **always -e-**; §10.3
expressly declares forms such as \**aulan* or \**eirdas* to be wrong.

Attested sentences: *Xla veiş dolvet xnaş şirneş xnan brasin.* (§25.1) ·
*Nunda melam vim dun xnaş breuneş vis.* (§25.1) ·
*mai traivam vim xlan eirden* (§16.2) ·
*Tel xraş navildoş milkot ro xlan luiven xlas eirdes.* (§25.2).

### 5.2 Stress and syllable structure

The 15 core words carry the stress on the **last** syllable (§23: *kaUN, breUN, taIV*) and
thus deviate from the normal rule "penultimate syllable".

Note on **K-01**: *aul* and *eird* (together with all inflected forms) have a vocalic
onset with a closed syllable (VK and VKK respectively). §5.1 does not list these syllable
shapes. That is a finding against the grammar, not an error of the words.

## 6. The ten-percent group (§10.1)

In these words the final vowel has been lost historically; the gender remains
recognizable from the remaining consonant.

| Modern | Meaning | Proto-Orbis | Explanation | Gender |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **velkran** | friendship | \*velkra-na | final vowel lost | *-n-* → neuter |
| **soralm** | monument | \*soral-mu | final vowel lost | *-m-* → feminine |
| **prilm** | craft | \*prila-mu | merger | *-m-* → feminine |

### 6.1 Declension in the singular

As with the core words, with linking vowel **-e-** (§10.1), attested on *velkran*:

| Case | Form |
|---|---|
| Nominative | velkran |
| Accusative | velkranen |
| Dative | velkraneş |
| Genitive | velkranes |

For these three words there is no example sentence in 0.9.3; the only attestation is the
form series *velkran, velkranen, velkraneş, velkranes* in §10.1. Test corpus 0.1 likewise
contains no sentence with *velkran*, *soralm* or *prilm*. The *velkraş* used in the
example sentence §17.3 belongs to the regular noun *velkra* "friend" (M-A), not to
*velkran*.

### 6.2 Plural

**[REGELLÜCKE] L-07:** §10.1 regulates only the singular. The regular plural (*-ñ-* after
the theme vowel) is not applicable for want of a theme vowel (\**velkranñ*), and the core
word plural **-ei** is according to §9 expressly reserved for the 15 core words. "The
friendships", "the monuments", "the crafts" **cannot be formed** in 0.9.3. Nothing is
guessed and nothing is added here.

## 7. The article (§11)

> Definiteness marker (**x-** definite, **v-** indefinite) + gender consonant of the
> **A series** (r / l / n) + **-a**, followed by the usual markers.

### 7.1 Definite article (§11.1)

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nom sg | **xra** | **xla** | **xna** |
| Acc sg | xran | xlan | xnan |
| Dat sg | xraş | xlaş | xnaş |
| Gen sg | xras | xlas | xnas |
| Nom pl | xrañ | xlañ | xnañ |
| Acc pl | xrañan | xlañan | xnañan |
| Dat pl | xrañaş | xlañaş | xnañaş |
| Gen pl | xrañas | xlañas | xnañas |

The echo vowel of the article is always **a** — even where the noun has a different theme
vowel: *xrañaş larkiñiş* (§25.2), *xnañan vrestoñon* (test corpus 0.1, test 067).

### 7.2 Indefinite article (§11.2)

**vra** (m) · **vla** (f) · **vna** (n), with the same markers: *vran, vraş, vras* etc.

> **In the plural there is no indefinite article** (§11.2).

Attestations: *Vra melru molet dral xrañan trelmrañan zaldreñen.* (§25.2) ·
*Vraş zaldreş traivot ro vnan aulen şlim xrañaş larkiñiş.* (§25.2) ·
*Tund vim vra vlaidra valru mai em …* (§16.2).

### 7.3 The article shows only the gender (§11.3)

The article **always** uses the A series. There are no articles with *k, d, v, m, s, t*.

| Noun | Subclass | Gender | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| valru | M-A | masculine | **xra** valru |
| narku | M-B | masculine | **xra** narku |
| vrondo | M-C | masculine | **xra** vrondo — *not* †xda |
| kirva | F-B | feminine | **xla** kirva |
| soruma | F-C | feminine | **xla** soruma |
| taisa | N-B | neuter | **xna** taisa |
| vresto | N-C | neuter | **xna** vresto |

With the 15 core words the article follows the dictionary gender (§11.3):
*xnaş şirneş* (to the child) · *xlan eirden* (the world, acc.) · *xnan aulen* (the water, acc.).

### 7.4 Formal marginal note

*vran* is at the same time indefinite article masculine accusative (§11.2) and the
particle "very" (§24.9). This homonymy is not a rule finding; it is recorded in phase 7 of
`Orbis-Testbericht-0_1.md`.

## 8. Compounds with a nominal head (§21.3)

Modifier in front, head word behind; gender and class follow the last member.

| Compound | Formation | Gender |
|---|---|---|
| *taivbreun* school | taiv + breun | n |
| *aulmelna* canal | aul + melna | n |
| *luivresto* calendar | luiv + vresto (junction rule §21.4) | n |

**[REGELUNKLARHEIT] U-08:** *taivbreun* has the core word *breun* as its head. Whether
the compound is declined like a core word (linking vowel *-e-*, plural *-ei*) or
regularly, §21.3 does not say — the core words have no class. *aulmelna* and *luivresto*
with regular heads are unproblematic.
