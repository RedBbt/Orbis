# ORBIS — The Adjective

*Derived from the German documentation, which is authoritative (see TRANSLATION_POLICY.md). Describes Orbis Grammar 0.9.3.*

---

## 1. Two states

In Orbis the adjective has two clearly separated states (§12):

| State | Form | Attestation |
|---|---|---|
| **attributive** — before the noun | stem + **r / l / n** + **a** + marker, agreeing | *xra vlaidra valru* (§12.1) |
| **predicative** — after *esex*, *vurnex*, *stanex* | **base form**, without any ending | *Lo loşn est.* (§12.2) |

The dictionary form of the adjective is the base form (§24.7): *vlaid*, not *vlaidra*.

---

## 2. Attributive (§12.1)

> **Stem + r / l / n + a + case/number marker**

The building block *r / l / n + a* is the A series of the class consonants (§7) — the same
one the article uses (§11.3). The adjective therefore shows **only the gender**, never the
subclass of the noun.

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nom sg | -ra | -la | -na |
| Acc sg | -ran | -lan | -nan |
| Dat sg | -raş | -laş | -naş |
| Gen sg | -ras | -las | -nas |
| Nom pl | -rañ | -lañ | -nañ |
| Acc pl | -rañan | -lañan | -nañan |
| Dat pl | -rañaş | -lañaş | -nañaş |
| Gen pl | -rañas | -lañas | -nañas |

In the plural the echo vowel of the adjective is always **a**, even when the noun has a
different theme vowel (U-09) — visible in *vlaidnañaş milneñeş*.

Attested combinations:

| Attestation | Structure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| *xra vlaidra valru* — the big man | M nom sg | §12.1 |
| *xlan vlaidlan sarlan* — the big woman (acc.) | F acc sg | §12.1 |
| *xnañaş vlaidnañaş milneñeş* — to the big eyes | N dat pl | §12.1 |
| *dral xrañan trelmrañan zaldreñen* — through the long days | M acc pl | §25.2 |
| *xra vlaidvaxra vrondo* — the biggest mountain | M nom sg, superlative | §12.3 |
| *xanra valru* — no man | M nom sg, *xan-* | §18.3 |
| *xlaş şalnlaş kavlaş* — to the dark city | F dat sg | test corpus 0.1, test 058 |
| *loşnla* | F nom sg | test corpus 0.1, test 048 |

Article, adjective and noun agree throughout in gender, case and number.

### 2.1 The junction rule on the adjective (§12.1, §21.4)

If the adjective stem ends in the same consonant with which the ending begins, the two
merge into one (§21.4):

| Stem | + ending | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| *şaln* | + *-na* | **şalna** | §12.1 |
| *girn* | + *-na* | **girna** | §12.1 |
| *xarn* | + *-na* | **xarna** | §12.1 |

With different consonants the junction is preserved: *şaln* + *-laş* → *şalnlaş* (test
corpus 0.1, test 058). Among the 20 basic adjectives (§24.7) none ends in *r* or *l*; in
attributive use the junction rule therefore applies only before the neuter *-na* and only
with the nine stems ending in *-n* (section 7).

## 3. Predicative (§12.2)

> After **esex** (to be), **vurnex** (to become) and **stanex** (to stay) the **base
> form** stands, without ending.

| Attestation | English | Source |
|---|---|---|
| *Lo loşn est.* — *not* †*Lo loşna est.* | She is beautiful. | §12.2 |
| *Xla kirva girn vot.* | The night was cold. | §12.2, §25.2 |
| *Xra valru xarn vurt.* | The man becomes strong. | §12.2 |
| *Xna breun granz stanat.* | The house stays old. | §12.2, §25.1 |
| *Xrañ larkiñ girn esten.* | The stones are cold. | §12.2 |
| *Xla luiv luid vot, xla kirva girn vot.* | The sun was bright, the night was cold. | §17.5, §25.2 |

The second-to-last attestation shows expressly: **in the plural too the base form remains
unchanged** (§12.2). The predicative is intensified by *vran* "very" (§24.9):
*grali xla kirva vran luid est* (§25.1).

The copula may not be omitted (§17.5); whether poetic omission will become admissible is
marked in §17.5 as **[NOCH ZU ENTSCHEIDEN]** (still to be decided).

**[REGELUNKLARHEIT]** (rule ambiguity) **U-13 — position of the predicative.** In all
examples of the grammar the predicative adjective stands **before** the finite verb (*Lo
loşn est* — the verb thus formally in position 3). This is in tension with the V2 rule
(§17.1). Whether predicative and copula together count as **one** sentence position — as
§16.2 expressly lays down for *mai* + verb — is stated by no rule (test corpus 0.1, test
055). Nothing is decided here.

Whether the list of copula verbs (*esex, vurnex, stanex*) is exhaustive, §12.2 does not
say.

## 4. Comparison (§12.3)

| Degree | Infix | attributive (m) | predicative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positive | — | vlaidra | vlaid |
| Comparative | **-vi-** | vlaidvira | vlaidvi |
| Superlative | **-vax-** | vlaidvaxra | vlaidvax |

The infix is inserted between stem and adjective ending; the ending itself remains
unchanged. With stems ending in *-v* the junction rule applies (§21.4): *selv* + *vi* →
**selvi**, *zilv* + *vira* → **zilvira** (§12.3).

**Words of comparison:** **kon** (than) · **zil** (as, like)

| Attestation | English | Source |
|---|---|---|
| *Ro vlaidvi est kon vim.* | He is bigger than I. | §12.3, test corpus 0.1 test 054 |
| *Lo loşn est zil luiv.* | She is beautiful like the sun. | §12.3 |
| *Xra vlaidvaxra vrondo* | the biggest mountain | §12.3 |

**[REGELUNKLARHEIT] U-06 — case after *kon* / *zil*.** Both attestations show the
nominative (*kon vim*, *zil luiv*); a rule on this is missing. With "I see him as a
friend" the case would be open. *kon* and *zil* stand in §24.9 among the particles, not in
the preposition list §19, which regulates case government.

## 5. Adverbs from adjectives (§12.4)

> Base form + **-un**

Attestations: *vlaidun*, *zilvun*, *loşnun* (§12.4); in a sentence *Lo melat zilvun.* —
She goes fast. (test corpus 0.1, test 059).

The ending is added to the base form, not to the attributive form; it is not a case marker
and agrees with nothing.

**[REGELKONFLIKT]** (rule conflict) **K-04 — *tolm* against *tolmun*.** The grammar's own
example sentence §25.1 reads literally *"Xla soruma xlas nauşes vran tolm vaşnat"* — "The
memory of time passes away very slowly" — and uses **tolm without -un** in an adverbial
function. According to §12.4 *tolmun* would have to stand there. Either the example
sentence is faulty, or there is an unwritten exception; neither can be resolved in 0.9.3.
Two attestations, two forms (test corpus 0.1, test 060).

## 6. Nominalization (§12.5)

| What is meant | Suffix | Class | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| male person | **-ru** | M-A | *vlaidru* — the big one (m.) |
| female person | **-la** | F-A | *vlaidla* — the big one (f.) |
| thing or concept | **-te** | N-C | *vlaidte* — the big thing |

The three suffixes are the same as in the formation of word families in §21.1 (*melru,
mela; talru, tala*). Nominalized adjectives are full nouns and are declined according to
§8 and §9.

**Participle:** With the participle, **-ut** merges with **-te** into **-ute** (§12.5):
*traivut* → **traivute** "that which is found" (N-C). Attestation in the text: *Kilna est
xna traivute.* — "This is that which is found." (§25.2).

**[REGELUNKLARHEIT] U-02 — participle attributive.** §14 defines the participle *-ut*,
§16.3 uses it predicatively (*şunargut est*), §12.5 nominalizes it. Whether it may be
declined **attributively** like an adjective ("the found book" → *xna traivutna vresto*?)
is stated neither by §12 nor by §14 (test corpus 0.1, test 051). Nothing is added here.

## 7. The 20 basic adjectives (§24.7)

The base form is at the same time the dictionary form and the predicative form. The column
"Junction rule" names the places where §21.4 applies; the forms named there are formed
according to §12.1/§12.3 unless they are expressly marked as attested.

| Base form | Meaning | Final sound | Junction rule (§21.4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **vlaid** | big | d | — (attested: *vlaidra, vlaidlan, vlaidnañaş*, §12.1) |
| **nirm** | small | m | — |
| **selv** | good | v | before *-vi-/-vax-*: *selv + vi* → **selvi** (attested, §12.3) |
| **morn** | bad | n | before *-na*: *morn + na* → *morna* |
| **loşn** | beautiful | n | before *-na*: *loşn + na* → *loşna* |
| **granz** | old | z | — |
| **zirv** | new | v | before *-vi-/-vax-* |
| **trelm** | long | m | — (attested: *trelmrañan*, §25.2) |
| **misn** | short | n | before *-na* |
| **velm** | warm | m | — |
| **girn** | cold | n | before *-na*: *girn + na* → **girna** (attested, §12.1) |
| **luid** | bright | d | — |
| **şaln** | dark | n | before *-na*: *şaln + na* → **şalna** (attested, §12.1) |
| **xarn** | strong | n | before *-na*: *xarn + na* → **xarna** (attested, §12.1) |
| **vresn** | weak | n | before *-na* |
| **zilv** | fast | v | before *-vi-/-vax-*: *zilv + vira* → **zilvira** (attested, §12.3) |
| **tolm** | slow | m | — (→ K-04, section 5) |
| **klaun** | true | n | before *-na* |
| **norv** | false | v | before *-vi-/-vax-* |
| **xan** | no, none | n | before *-na*; attributively attested as *xanra valru* (§18.3) |

Notes:

- **xan** is at the same time the attributive word of negation (§18.3): *xanra valru* "no
  man". The particle negation *xa* before the finite verb (§18.3) is to be distinguished
  from it, as is the prefix *xa-* "opposite, absence" (§21.2, *xaselvra*).
- Nine of the 20 stems end in *-n* (*morn, loşn, misn, girn, şaln, xarn, vresn, klaun,
  xan*); only with these does the junction rule apply attributively, and only before the
  neuter ending *-na*. Four end in *-v* (*selv, zirv, zilv, norv*); with these it applies
  before the comparison infixes.
- Further adjective base forms arise productively with **-vi** (§21.1: *melvi* "eager to
  travel", *talvi* "talkative") and with the prefix **su-** "half" (§21.2: *suluidra*).
  *klaun* "true" is at the same time the base of the abstract noun *klaunuma* "truth"
  (§24.6).
